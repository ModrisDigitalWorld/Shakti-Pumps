1. WEBSITE DESIGN DIRECTION
Overall Look
Premium • Modern • Industrial • Clean • Trustworthy
The website should feel like an established engineering/industrial brand.
Visual Language
•	Deep professional blue
•	White background
•	Dark charcoal/grey typography
•	Large product photography
•	Clean grids
•	Sharp but subtle cards
•	Minimal shadows
•	Plenty of white space
•	Smooth, restrained animations
Design Principle
The website should look expensive because it is clean, not because it contains excessive graphics.
Avoid
•	Too many colours
•	Heavy gradients
•	Flashy animations
•	Overcrowded pages
•	Excessive text
•	Low-quality product images
•	Unnecessary stock photographs
•	Fake awards/certificates
•	Unsupported claims
•	Repeated information
